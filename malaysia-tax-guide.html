First Time Filing? Here's Your Complete Roadmap.
Follow these 6 steps in order. By the end, you'll know exactly what to do — and won't miss a single ringgit of relief.
STEP 01
Do I Need to File Tax?
💡
You must file if your annual income exceeds RM 34,000 after EPF — or RM 38,202 before EPF. (~RM 2,833/month take-home)

After EPF · Form BE
