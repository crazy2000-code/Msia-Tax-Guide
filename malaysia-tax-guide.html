STEP 01
Do I Need to File?
Check if you're required to register and file income tax in Malaysia.
💡
In Malaysia, you must register and file if your annual income exceeds RM 34,000 after EPF deductions — or RM 38,202 before EPF.

STEP 02
Register for a Tax File Number
Before you can file, you need a Tax Identification Number (TIN) — also called No. Cukai Pendapatan.
🆔
Already have a tax file number? Skip to Step 3. Check your MyTax login — if you can log in, you're already registered!
How to Register
1
Online via MyTax Portal (Easiest)
Go to mytax.hasil.gov.my → Click "Daftar" (Register) → Choose "Individu" → Fill in your MyKad details. Your TIN is created instantly!
🔗 mytax.hasil.gov.my
2
Via e-Daftar (Alternative)
Go to LHDN's e-Daftar system. Fill in your IC number and personal details. You'll receive your TIN via email or SMS.
3
Walk-in at LHDN Office
Bring your MyKad (original + copy) to the nearest LHDN (Inland Revenue Board) office. Staff will register you on the spot.
✅
Tip: If your employer has been deducting PCB (Monthly Tax Deduction) from your salary, you're likely already registered. Ask your HR or payroll team for your tax file number!

STEP 04
Log In & Fill the e-Form
Go to MyTax portal and fill in your income, deductions, and relief claims. Here's exactly what to do.
1
Go to MyTax Portal
Visit mytax.hasil.gov.my and log in with your TIN (tax file number) + IC number + password.
🔗 mytax.hasil.gov.my
2
Select "e-Filing" → "e-BE" or "e-B"
Choose e-BE if you're a salaried employee. Choose e-B if you have business/self-employment income. Select Year of Assessment 2024.
3
Fill in Part A — Personal Info
Your name, IC, address, marital status, bank account number (for refund). Most fields are auto-filled — just verify they're correct.
4
Fill in Part B — Income
Enter your total employment income (from EA Form). For self-employed: enter gross business income minus allowable expenses. Also include any rental income, dividends, or other income.
5
Fill in Part C — Tax Reliefs
This is the most important part! Enter all your eligible reliefs — EPF, insurance, lifestyle, medical, education, children, etc. Use our Tax Relief Checklist for reference.
6
Fill in Part D — Tax Rebates
Enter your PCB/MTD amount (from payslip or EA Form). Also include zakat payments if applicable — these are direct rebates, not just relief!
7
Review the Summary
The system will calculate your tax payable automatically. You'll see either a refund amount (+) or balance payable (−). Double-check everything before submitting!
🏦
Make sure to enter your correct bank account number in the personal info section. If you have a refund, LHDN will bank it in directly — usually within 30–90 days.
STEP 05
Submit & Pay (If Any)
You're almost done! Submit your form and settle any balance — or wait for your refund.

Payment Methods
FPX Online Banking (Recommended)
Pay directly via MyTax portal using your online banking. Instant confirmation. Available 24/7.
✓ Instant
ByrHASIL Portal
Visit byrhasil.hasil.gov.my. Pay using credit/debit card, online banking, or even at ATMs.
byrhasil.hasil.gov.my
Cheque / Bank Draft
Made payable to "Ketua Pengarah Hasil Dalam Negeri". Submit at any LHDN office or drop box.
⏱ Allow 3–5 days
⚠️
Late payment penalty: 10% of tax payable for the first month, increasing to 25%+ thereafter. Always pay by the deadline even if you need to estimate!
